## For tutors
The idea of this lectures (1, 2) is for the students to see you working with the devtools and your thought process, it's a good opportunity to get a "hands on" experience turning a project they are familiar with into a responsive design

Get started:
- Download the [canonical version of the project](https://github.com/tripleten-com/se-canonicals_en/tree/html/sprint-1-new_stage-3) 
- Open project on vscode
- Good luck coding along with the students

Tips:
- If they don't seem to have any questions, ask yourself some questions, `how would you approach this`, `Does this look ok?`
- Follow some of their suggestions even if they are not right, show why they are not right
