## Step 2 Go to settings/pages

2.1 scroll to the top of the Settings page, and click the **Pages** button on the sidebar.![[Image (4).png]]

---

In the GitHub Pages settings, choose the following options:

- For **Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**
- For **Branch,** choose `main` and `/ (root)`, and then click **Save**
![[Image (5).png]]
---
After this, your page will be deployed to a URL with the following format:

```
https://username.github.io/repo-name
```

Usually, the project is published on GitHub Pages instantly. However, it can sometimes take up to 20 minutes.  <!-- element class="fragment" -->

If your site doesn’t open at the link you’re given, try adding `index.html` at the end of the URL. For example: <!-- element class="fragment" -->
```
https://username.github.io/repo-name/index.html
```
