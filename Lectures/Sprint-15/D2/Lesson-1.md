# Deploying and Hosting in the Cloud

---

## Why move to the Cloud?

- **Local servers** are self-managed, but require handling security, networking, and stability.
<!-- element class="fragment" -->
- **Cloud servers** are ready-to-use, managed by a provider, and can be set up in minutes.
<!-- element class="fragment" -->
- Popular providers include AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure. We'll use **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**.

---

## Google Cloud Platform (GCP)

- **Free Tier:** GCP offers a free tier perfect for our project.

  - New users get a ~$300 credit for 3 months.
  - After that, an "always free" tier is available for small applications.

- We will create a Virtual Machine (VM) that fits within this free tier.
